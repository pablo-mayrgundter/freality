How do I get my archive?
  1. On x.com: Settings → Your account → Download an archive of your data.
  2. Verify, request, then wait (minutes to ~24h) for the "your archive is ready" mail.
  3. Download the ZIP and drop it above. It contains every tweet you've posted.

Don't want to wait? See scrape.js for a console snippet that scrapes tweets from a profile/search page live (paste it into your browser console, like the spam tool), then drop the JSON it saves here.
